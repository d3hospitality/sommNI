Privacy policy

wineLENS is a D3 Hospitality project for exploring wine, keeping a private collection and practising recall on Even G2 and its phone companion. This page explains the data used by the beta.

Your account and collection

Google sign-in supplies your account identifier, email and basic profile details, such as your name and avatar. We do not request access to your Gmail or contacts. Supabase stores your profile, bottles, vintages, personal notes, uploaded bottle photos and account study reviews. Your collection belongs to your account; it is not published as part of the shared wine catalog.

Linking your glasses

A short-lived code connects an Even Hub installation to your account. The server stores a hash of the code, its expiry and linked-device records, including a generic label, link time and last-seen time. We do not use a hardware serial number for account linking. To limit code guessing, the API records keyed hashes of network addresses. Raw addresses and codes are not stored in the pairing tables. Attempt records are eligible for removal after a day during code issuance.

On your device

The website saves a sign-in session in browser storage. The companion stores its linked session in Even Hub storage, or localStorage when used in a browser. Favorites, stock, pairings and local study history also use device storage. Signed-out study is local. Account review history syncs when linked. Unlinking clears the current account’s Winebrary cache and study log; independent local companion data is separate.

Photos, voice and service providers

Only photos you choose are uploaded for your Winebrary. If you explicitly request a studio bottle rendering, the selected reference image is processed by the image-generation service used by the wineLENS API. Review the generated image before saving it. Voice search starts when you request it and uses the app’s speech-processing service; account linking itself does not access your microphone.

The beta uses Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Vercel for the website and APIs; Google for sign-in; and Even Hub for the companion host. These providers process the data needed to deliver their parts of the service. Infrastructure providers may maintain operational and security logs under their own retention policies. The website does not include advertising trackers or marketing analytics.

Payments and label scans

Stripe processes subscription and token payments. We store Stripe customer, subscription and payment-session identifiers, subscription status, billing periods, allowance usage, spending preferences and an append-only token ledger. Card details are entered on Stripe and are not stored by wineLENS.

When you request a label scan, your selected photo is sent to OpenAI to extract wine details and generate a clearly marked draft tasting note. It is not saved as a bottle photo automatically. The server keeps a photo fingerprint and extracted result to prevent duplicate charges and recover a repeated request; it does not store the scan photo. Review the result before saving it to your private Winebrary. OpenAI also processes explicitly requested Studio renderings. Providers handle request data under their own retention policies.

Removing your data

Use your account page to revoke devices or delete your account. Account deletion removes your authentication user, profile, private collection, cloud bottle files, account study reviews and device-link records. Scan results, usage counters, wallet and spending preferences are also removed. Shared reference wines and their source provenance remain. Financial ledger records, containing account identifiers but no email or photo, are retained for payment reconciliation, disputes and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Stripe retains payment records under its policies. Contact us about retained records. Deletion is permanent. Copies in provider backups may remain until those backups expire.

A revoked session can no longer refresh. An already-issued access token can remain valid until its expiry. The companion checks link validity on use, on return to the app and periodically while active. Offline devices cannot receive deletion immediately; their cached account data clears when the app reconnects and validates the account. Unlink on those devices to clear it locally.

Questions or requests

Contact ops@d3hospitality.com about access, correction, export or deletion, including if you cannot sign in. Send the request from your account email where possible; do not send passwords or link codes.
