Terms of use

These terms cover the wineLENS beta website, account linking and companion experience provided by D3 Hospitality. By using an account, you agree to these terms.

Free to use, tokens when you want more

wineLENS is free. The catalog and its tasting notes, Atlas, Study, Find My Wine, adding wines by hand, your own notes and your own bottle photo are free. Each account also receives 3 welcome tokens once, for its first wine cards. Free use includes, per calendar month UTC, five label scans, five drafted tasting notes, three sommelier picks, one wine-list photo page and 20 wine-list text pages. Wine lists uploaded as spreadsheets (CSV) are read without AI and are free. Your first token pack unlocks larger monthly allowances for the life of the service: 60 label scans, 60 drafted tasting notes, 30 sommelier picks, 20 wine-list photo pages and 100 wine-list text pages. Unlocked allowances reset monthly on the anniversary of that first purchase and do not roll over. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Checkout shows the charge before you authorize it.

Tokens and refunds

Tokens are sold in USD through Stripe with no subscription: $5 for 20, $10 for 45, $15 for 70, $20 for 100 or $50 for 275. One wine card uses one token every time it is made, including when wineLENS reuses an image or notes it already made for the same bottle and vintage. A wine card is a bottle image (made from your label photo when you have uploaded one, otherwise drawn from the wine's details with an original label that does not reproduce the producer's artwork), tasting notes (unless you wrote your own), the vintage and a place on your map. A new bottle image uses one token. After your allowance, a label scan, drafted tasting notes, sommelier picks, a wine-list photo page or a wine-list text page (about 6,000 characters of a PDF or pasted list) uses one token each. When a wine list has more pages than your remaining allowance, the allowance covers what it can and the rest uses tokens. A wine list in which no wines are found is not charged. Tokens are reserved before processing, committed after success and returned on failure. Token spending requires confirmation unless you enable Always allow. Tokens never expire. Tokens have no cash value and are not transferable. Deleting your account forfeits remaining tokens, subject to applicable refund rights.

Purchases are generally non-refundable once used. Contact ops@d3hospitality.com about unused purchases, billing errors or refund requests. Statutory refund and cancellation rights remain unaffected. Refunds are reviewed by support; there is no automatic refund flow in the app. Features and future prices may change with notice; existing token reservations use the rate in effect when reserved.

Using wineLENS

The glasses experience requires Even G2 and the Even app. Google sign-in takes place in your browser; an eight-character code links your account to the companion. Keep codes and device sessions private. Only link devices you control. You are responsible for content you upload and for having permission to use it.

Your collection and content

You retain your rights in your photos and personal notes. You allow us and our service providers to store and process that content to provide the features you request, including bottle rendering when you select it. Your private collection is separate from the shared reference catalog. Do not upload unlawful material or attempt to access another person’s account.

Reference information

Tasting notes, wine information and study prompts are for general reference. Sources may be incomplete or change. OpenAI label scans and drafted tasting notes may be inaccurate. Drafted notes describe the expected profile of a wine from what is known about it; they are not a tasting. They are drafted once per producer, wine and vintage: if wineLENS already has them, you get them without using an allowance or tokens, and only the first request for that bottle and vintage is charged. Neither is a substitute for reading the label or tasting the wine. Review every field before saving. Wines read from wine lists can be misread or matched to the wrong place; you review every wine before it is saved. When you save a wine that is new to wineLENS, its producer, name, place, grape and style may be suggested to the shared catalog; wineLENS reviews each suggestion before it becomes a catalog wine, and approved catalog wines are part of the shared reference service. Generated bottle images can contain errors; compare labels and vintages with your original photograph. Simulator captures and 3D composites illustrate the app and do not guarantee how a display will appear in every environment.

Availability and account closure

Keep your own copies of information that matters to you. We may suspend access for abuse or security reasons. You can unlink devices or permanently delete your account through the account page. See the privacy policy for what is removed and how offline devices are handled.

Responsible use

Use wineLENS in a way that is appropriate for your age and local rules. It does not provide medical advice or encourage alcohol consumption. Use glasses only where it is safe to read a display.
