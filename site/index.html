A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before
you pour.

Is wineLENS free?

Yes. The catalog, tasting notes, Atlas, Study, manual add and your own bottle photos are free. Free includes five label scans each month. Pro adds larger allowances and optional tokens.

What are tokens?

Optional top-ups for Pro. Included allowances are used first, then tokens only with your consent or Always allow enabled. A failed generation returns its allowance or tokens.

Do tokens expire? Can I get a refund?

Tokens never expire. Purchases are generally non-refundable once used; contact ops@d3hospitality.com about unused purchases or billing errors. Statutory refund rights remain unaffected. See our terms.

What happens without Pro?

Your saved wines and free features remain available. Tokens stay in your account but cannot be purchased or spent until Pro is active again. Deleting your account forfeits the balance, subject to applicable refund rights.

What do I need?

Even G2 glasses and the Even app for the glasses experience. The phone companion runs inside Even Hub. Use a regular browser for Google sign-in.

Is my collection private?

Your Winebrary is tied to your account. Other users cannot browse it. The shared wine reference catalog is separate from your own bottles, photos and notes.

What data is stored?

Your Google account email and profile, saved bottles and photos, tasting notes, study reviews and linked-device records. The app also keeps local companion data. See our privacy policy for the full picture.

Can I use it without an account?

You can explore the reference catalog, Atlas and local study. Link an account to save a private Winebrary and sync study reviews. An internet connection is needed for account linking and cloud features.

Can I unlink a device or delete my account?

Yes. Sign in on the link page to revoke a device or confirm account deletion. Deletion removes your account, private collection, cloud photos and account study reviews. It cannot be undone. Locally saved data on offline devices clears when the app reconnects and checks the account.
