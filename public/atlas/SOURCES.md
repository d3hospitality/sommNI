# wineLENS Atlas sources

Country geography: [Natural Earth](), public domain. The 1:50m admin-0 dataset includes countries and territories. Borders are simplified, not a statement of political recognition.

Winery locations and region groupings: [Oliver Dressler / winerymap](), MIT, data collected August–October 2024. License retained alongside this file. Region centers are calculated from source points; they are not legal appellation centroids or polygons. Winery counts describe this dataset, not all operating wineries.

Pinned source URLs, hashes and exclusions are in atlas-data.json. No external tiles, live location permission, tracking, or runtime geocoding.

## Original wineLENS dot treatment — 16 October 2026

`src/atlas/dot-atlas.ts` is a new point-field rendering implementation. It samples the existing Natural Earth country-ID mask, then draws an equal-area dotted globe and a local projected dot map. It does not render borrowed map tiles or the previous atlas raster output. The land samples, star placement, visual hierarchy, region locators, leader lines and enlarged point inset are generated by this renderer.

Land dots are geographic samples, not wineries. Region rings identify the calculated source-cluster center, not vineyard addresses or appellation boundaries. The map inset expands the source coordinates to reveal distribution; its scale differs from the country-context map. Source coverage is incomplete and dates to 2024. No additional data was scraped or inferred.

## Interactive country and region boundaries

`boundaries.json` adds Natural Earth admin-0 countries and admin-1 states/provinces/departments at pinned commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`. Source URLs and SHA-256 values are retained in that file. `scripts/build-atlas-boundaries.py` reproduces the output. Polygons are simplified for display; administrative borders are not wine appellation borders. Counts are winery source points inside the original, unsimplified administrative polygons. Source wine-area groups can cross those borders. Catalogue wine associations use existing region-to-source-group links, not precise vineyard addresses. History is shown only where an existing sourced card matches the selection.

### Italian regional grouping correction — beta 3.2.3

Natural Earth's Italian admin-1 records are provinces. The build now dissolves their original polygons by the source `region_cod` and `region` fields into the 20 parent regions before simplification. This makes Piedmont (Piemonte), Tuscany (Toscana), and the other Italian regions selectable and removes internal province edges from their highlighted outlines. English/Italian display aliases are retained together where useful. Winery counts and source-group associations are recalculated against the merged polygons.

To reproduce: install `shapely==2.0.7` in a Python virtual environment, then run `python scripts/build-atlas-boundaries.py`. Shapely is a build-time dependency only; it is not shipped to the browser.
