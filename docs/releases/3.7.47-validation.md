# sommNI 3.7.47 beta

## Origin hierarchy

Shared canonical display/grouping resolves country aliases, removes repeated region tokens, and separates recognized US states from wine regions. Napa Valley, Napa Valley AVA, and Napa Valley + California/CA group as Napa Valley with California context. Napa County, Sonoma Valley and explicit appellations stay distinct. Original source strings and Supabase records are preserved; this is a common browsing projection, not a destructive database rewrite. Japanese prefecture aliases remain supported.

## Atlas and glasses

Bottle selection uses a shared location resolver. Phone maps ease toward the known regional winery cluster with state/province outlines and a gold area glow; the wine deck remains unchanged while browsing. Country/region controls still filter explicitly. Unknown regions fall back to administrative or country context. Winery clusters are approximate context, not exact AVA polygons or vineyard positions.

The G2 scene frames the region/state instead of the country midpoint. Existing two image tiles and serialized transport are retained; no continuous glasses animation added. Scrolling updates the caption as well as the bottle/map, with stale-selection guards and no page rebuild.
